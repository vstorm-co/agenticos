# AgenticOS vs Wonderful

An enterprise purchase includes deployment and operating work around the software. Wonderful presents an AI platform with deployment teams. AgenticOS can be operated by your team, with implementation help from Vstorm scoped separately.

Maintained by the AgenticOS team at Vstorm. Sources checked 25 September 2026. AgenticOS baseline: v0.0.503. Wonderful scope: public AI OS and deployment pages, not a negotiated contract or tested account.

## Define what you are buying

[Wonderful AI OS](https://www.wonderful.ai/ai-os) lists multi-tenant SaaS, single-tenant and bring-your-own-cloud deployment options. Its [deployment offering](https://www.wonderful.ai/deployment) describes delivery teams. Verify availability and terms for your engagement. Owned infrastructure is therefore not, by itself, a unique AgenticOS distinction.

With AgenticOS, the operator owns the deployment. Vstorm can discuss client-infrastructure installation, documentation, process creation and custom development. Support, maintenance, integrations and response commitments are agreed for the project; they are not included automatically with the repository.

## Questions for the delivery agreement

| Area | Settle before the pilot |
| --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Where does each component run, and who updates and restores it? |
| Data and access | Which services receive data, and who maintains identities and credentials? |
| Process | Who defines the task, handles exceptions and accepts its results? |
| Support | Who handles incidents, with what agreed coverage? |
| Exit | What code, configuration and data can the client retain or export? |

Choose a delivered platform when its scope and responsibilities match your requirements. Evaluate AgenticOS with Vstorm when an owned deployment and custom work fit the project. No enterprise-capability equivalence, SLA or client outcome is claimed here.
