Graphics in docs/assets/readme/, shown by README.md and its translations

The figures are rendered from figures.html beside this file (render.mjs); the
product screens inside the annotated ones are captured from a test deployment
by capture.mjs. Figures are test records, not benchmarks.

Agent faces in every figure:
  Drawn by blobatar (https://www.npmjs.com/package/blobatar), the library the
  console draws agent avatars with; MIT. Illustrations, not real people.

Icons:
  Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC.

Type, rendered into the images:
  Instrument Serif, Inter and Geist Mono, from Google Fonts; SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Brand marks in figures.webp, eight-surfaces.webp, model-providers.webp and integrations-hub.webp:
  Reused from frontend/src/lib/brand-glyphs.generated.ts (Simple Icons CC0 1.0,
  Font Awesome Free CC BY 4.0, @lobehub/icons MIT). The marks belong to their owners
  and identify connection options; they do not imply a partnership or endorsement.
