# Product messaging

Primary reader: a technical team owner or integrator with a repeated document or tool-based task and an operating owner for a self-hosted deployment.

Promise: **Put AI agents to work on your team's tasks.** Explain it with: build agents in your browser, connect documents and tools, and run them on infrastructure you control.

Lead with the task and a checkable result. Use the first-document tutorial as the primary CTA. Explain the platform and OS architecture after the reader understands the work. Distinguish documented capabilities from executed demonstrations and proposals.

Never imply self-hosted means fully offline, zero budget overshoot, universal approval coverage or inherited source ACLs. Do not borrow client outcomes from unrelated Vstorm projects. Vstorm implementation and maintenance scope is agreed separately.

The primary campaign objective is AgenticOS recognition and adoption. Vstorm implementation and custom-project leads are a downstream benefit. Reuse the maintained comparison guides, operating page and help route rather than duplicating claims in campaign copy.
