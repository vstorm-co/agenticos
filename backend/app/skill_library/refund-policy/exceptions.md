# Exceptions to the 30-day rule

These override the date, and can be applied without approval.

## Faulty goods

Within the statutory warranty, a fault present at delivery is refundable
regardless of the order date. Ask for a photograph; do not ask for the original
packaging.

## Late delivery

If the carrier's tracking shows delivery more than 7 days after the promised
date, refund the shipping cost without being asked. Refund the order in full
if the customer says it arrived too late to be useful.

## Subscription charged after cancellation

Refund in full and cancel again, checking that the cancellation actually took.
This is our fault twice: once for the charge, once for the cancellation that
did not stick.

## Goodwill

Up to the goodwill limit, once per customer, at the agent's discretion - for
example a long support thread that should have been one message. Record it as
goodwill so it does not read as an admission that the original charge was wrong.

## Never an exception

- A change of mind on a personalised or made-to-order item.
- A digital item that has been downloaded, unless it is faulty.
- An order placed with a discount code that has since been withdrawn.
