# <Short title: what broke, for whom>

**Severity:** SEV1 / SEV2 / SEV3
**Detected:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC - by <alert / customer report / someone looking>
**Resolved:** YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC
**Duration of impact:** <minutes>

## Impact

Who was affected and what they could not do. Numbers where there are numbers:
requests failed, orders not placed, customers who wrote in.

## Timeline

| Time (UTC) | What happened |
|------------|---------------|

Include when it started, when anybody noticed, when the cause was understood,
and when impact ended. Those four are the ones every reader looks for.

## Cause

What actually happened, mechanically. Say which part is established and which
is believed.

## What made it worse

Anything that lengthened it: an alert nobody owned, a runbook that was wrong, a
dashboard that showed green.
