# School, play and work

Adapt visual form to the question. A concept explanation needs a different rhythm from a business comparison or a piece of procedural art. Use an existing brand when provided, and choose a deliberate direction when it is open.

For school and learning, start from a concrete situation the learner recognizes. Put a labelled model beside a short explanation; let a control reveal one relationship at a time. Distinguish the learner's prediction from the model's result and explain mistakes without shaming. State assumptions, units and the model's limits. Give the person a useful static explanation before requiring interaction.

For play, make the rules, goal, progress and reset understandable. Use original geometry, strong contrast and expressive typography where they serve the activity. Keep motion optional, pause continuous animation, and respect reduced motion. Avoid flashing, autoplay sound or timers that prevent reading. Seed procedural randomness when repeatability matters. An artistic field or a simulated experiment is not observational data.

For work, lead with what the viewer needs to decide or understand. Compare on shared scales, keep source/date/uncertainty near the relevant quantity, and expose assumptions in a scenario model. Prefer a readable chart or diagram over an ornamental 3D scene. Make the output useful when printed or viewed without hover, and include a table or textual alternative for exact values.

## Vary composition, not just accent color

Possible directions include a light editorial explainer with serif headings, a dark technical model with crisp labels, a colorful classroom activity, a playful illustrated simulation, or a restrained comparison with controls beside consequences. Change hierarchy, spacing, imagery and composition to fit the subject. Use semantic theme tokens; test both text and visual marks for contrast. System fonts and local libraries work offline; a remote font or guessed CDN does not.

Use real image-generation capability for photographic/painterly subjects when available. SVG/Canvas is better for precise labels, diagrams, charts and reproducible geometry. Do not draw fake photographs from hundreds of arbitrary CSS elements. Do not put exact numbers or critical labels inside generated raster images when they can remain selectable text.

## Deliver on the destination

Inside `/chat`, the drawing can be interactive. In Mattermost and other channels, HTML is a link or attachment, not an embedded running app. Use the runtime's available rendered image/file mechanism, or publish a reusable App and send its authenticated link. Verify the actual delivery; do not claim a room can see a private preview just because you can. A static export must preserve the explanation and labels without hidden hover state. Keep the live version for meaningful interaction and the PDF/image for the requested static use.
