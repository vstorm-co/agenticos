Where the friction lives

Most observed delays happen at handoffs, where a clear owner matters more than another automation.

Inspect the evidence

Evidence ledger

  1. Handoffs take the longest

    Example process review: median 9 hours across 24 handoffs. Small sample; one unusually busy week.

  2. Ownership reduces rework

    Illustrative interview notes: 6 of 8 participants wanted an explicit next owner.

  3. A two-team pilot is representative

    This is an assumption to validate, not an observed result. Include teams with different workloads.

Limitations and next check

Replace these examples with attributable source records, dates and sample sizes. Compare handoff times before and after the pilot; review whether the teams represent the wider organization.
