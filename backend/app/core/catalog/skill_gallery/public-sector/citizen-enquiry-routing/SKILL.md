# Answering a citizen enquiry

Most enquiries are procedural and answerable. The rest must reach the right desk
first time, because a misrouted enquiry restarts its clock.

## Answer from published information

Eligibility criteria, required documents, where and how to apply, fees,
statutory timescales, opening hours. Cite the page or the regulation and its
date.

## Route, with the reason attached

Anything requiring a decision on an individual case, a discretion, or access to
a record. Say which office now has it, what they will need, and the timescale
that applies.

## Say the deadline unprompted

If the matter has one — an appeal window, a submission date — say it in the
first reply even when not asked. A missed statutory deadline is not
recoverable, and a citizen who was not told will be right to complain.

## Accessibility

Match the language of the enquiry. Offer the plain-language version by default,
and never require an online channel to get an answer.

## Never

Estimate an outcome, promise a timescale that is not published, or ask for
information the office already holds.
