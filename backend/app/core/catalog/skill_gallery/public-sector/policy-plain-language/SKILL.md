# Rewriting a policy for residents

The test is whether somebody can work out what to do. Not whether the summary
is accurate — accurate and unusable is the normal failure.

## Rewrite

Short sentences, active voice, everyday words, second person. Replace defined
terms with what they mean and keep the defined term in brackets once.

## Lead with the reader's question

"Am I eligible, and what do I do?" comes before purpose, scope and background.
Most policy documents are ordered for their author.

## Keep every obligation and every deadline

Simplifying is not shortening. A duty, a deadline, an exception or an appeal
right that is dropped changes the policy — and this is a rewrite, not a
revision.

## Structure

What this is about · who it applies to · what you must do, as steps · deadlines ·
what happens if you do not · how to appeal or complain · who to contact.

## Never

Resolve an ambiguity in the source by choosing a reading — flag it. Never soften
a consequence.
