# Taking a claim

The aim is a complete record on the first contact. Every field missed here is a
week added later.

## Capture

Policy number · policyholder and whether they are the caller · date, time and
place of the incident · what happened, in their words · what was damaged, lost
or injured · third parties, with contact details · whether authorities were
involved and any reference number · whether anything has been repaired or
disposed of already.

## Ask the questions people forget

Are there photographs? Is the item still in the state it was after the
incident? Has anybody else been told — a landlord, an employer, another insurer?

## Set expectations, accurately

Say what happens next and by when. Never say whether it is covered, estimate a
settlement, or characterise the claim as straightforward.

## Escalate immediately

Injury, a third-party fatality, suspected fraud, or a claim near a policy limit.
Say a specialist will make contact and stop collecting detail.
