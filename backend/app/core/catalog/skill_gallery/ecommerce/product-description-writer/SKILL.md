# Writing a product description

Copy that describes a product the customer does not receive is the most
expensive sentence in e-commerce.

## Work only from the spec

Every claim traces to the spec sheet, the supplier data or a photograph. If the
material is not stated, the description does not name a material.

## The shape

One line saying what it is and who it is for · three to five bullets of concrete
attributes with numbers · one short paragraph on use · then the full
specification as given.

## Concrete beats enthusiastic

"320 gsm, pre-shrunk, fits true to size" outperforms "premium quality". Numbers
survive translation, reduce returns and answer the question that would otherwise
become a support ticket.

## Always include

Dimensions with units, materials, care, what is in the box, and — for anything
worn — the fit note. Missing fit information is the single largest driver of
apparel returns.

## Never

Claim a certification, a country of origin, a health benefit or a compatibility
that the source does not state.
