# Answering "where is my order"

Seven in ten support messages are this one. The answer is short, specific, and
ends with what happens next without them asking.

## Always give three things

1. **Where it is now**, in plain words rather than carrier jargon — "left our
   warehouse Tuesday, scanned in Hamburg this morning".
2. **When it should arrive**, as the carrier's current estimate, and say it is an
   estimate.
3. **What happens next**, including what you will do if it does not move.

## When tracking has not updated

Say how long a gap is normal for that service before saying anything else. Most
"lost" parcels are a scan gap. Give the day on which you would call it late, and
offer to check back then.

## When it is genuinely late

Do not make them ask twice. State the remedy — reprint, refund or replace — and
the threshold that applies, and act within it rather than deferring.

## Never

Blame the carrier, promise a delivery date the carrier has not given, or ask
them to wait without saying how long.
