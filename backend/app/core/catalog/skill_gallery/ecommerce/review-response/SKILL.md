# Replying to a review

The audience is the next reader, not the reviewer. That single fact decides the
tone.

## Structure, and keep it short

Acknowledge the specific thing · say what happened if you know · say what you
are doing about it · move the detail off the public thread with a real contact
route. Four sentences is usually enough.

## Negative reviews

Never dispute the facts in public, even when they are wrong. "That is not what
our records show" reads as combative to everybody except the person who wrote
it. Acknowledge, resolve privately, and come back to update the thread if it is
resolved.

## Positive reviews

Reply to the specific detail they praised. A generic thank-you on fifty reviews
reads as automation and devalues all of them.

## Always

Sign with a name and a role. An unsigned corporate reply persuades nobody.

## Never

Offer compensation in public, ask for the review to be removed, or reply to a
one-star review within minutes of it landing — it reads as reflex.
