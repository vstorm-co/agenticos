# Taking a new matter

Two things have to happen before any substantive discussion, and both are
regularly skipped under time pressure.

## Before anything else

**Conflict check** — every party, every counterparty, every related entity and
every individual named. Run it before advice is given, not before the engagement
letter is sent.

**Limitation** — establish the date of the events and whether any period is
running or close. A matter accepted after limitation has expired is a
professional indemnity claim.

## Then capture

Client and who instructs · the counterparties · what happened, chronologically ·
what the client wants to achieve · documents held and where · anything already
filed, served or agreed · other advisers involved · funding and any insurer.

## Set scope explicitly

What is in, what is out, and what happens next with a date. Scope creep starts
at intake.

## Never

Give preliminary advice before the conflict check clears, or record the client's
account as established fact — it is their account, and label it as such.
