# First-pass document review

This produces a structured extract and a list of deviations. It is not advice
and it does not conclude.

## Extract, with a clause reference for each

Parties and their capacity · term, renewal and notice · payment terms · scope of
the obligations each way · liability caps and exclusions · indemnities ·
termination rights, each side · governing law and jurisdiction · assignment and
change of control · confidentiality and its survival period.

## Flag deviations, not preferences

Compare against the standard position and report where it differs, what the
difference is, and which way it cuts. "Liability cap is 6 months' fees against a
standard 12" — not "the cap is too low".

## Flag separately

Anything missing that the standard has, defined terms used but never defined,
cross-references that point nowhere, and inconsistent dates or figures between
clauses.

## Never

Advise, conclude a clause is acceptable, characterise risk as low, or redraft.
Every output is checked by the fee earner who signs it.
