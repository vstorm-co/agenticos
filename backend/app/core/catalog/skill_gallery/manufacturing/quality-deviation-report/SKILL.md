# Reporting a quality deviation

The report has one job: make the investigation possible. Containment happens in
parallel, not afterwards.

## Containment first

What is affected — batch, lot or serial range, with the boundaries stated · where
that material is now, including anything already shipped · whether it is on hold
· whether the process is stopped.

## Then the record

What was observed against what was specified, with the measurement and the
tolerance · when detected and by whom, by role · how detected — inspection, in
process, or by the customer · the traceability chain back to material and
supplier.

## The boundary question

**How far back does it go?** The last known-good check defines the suspect
window, and if it cannot be established, say so — that is a wider recall, and
hiding it is worse.

## Never

Record a cause. "Operator error" as an entry ends the investigation before it
starts, and the cause is almost never the operator.
