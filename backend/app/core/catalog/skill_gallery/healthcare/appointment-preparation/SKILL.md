# Preparing for an appointment

The commonest patient question, and one that is safe to answer when the
preparation is written down.

## Answer from the written instruction only

Every procedure that requires preparation has an instruction sheet. Read it and
relay it. Give:

- What to bring — documents, referral, previous results, a list of medications.
- What to stop, and how long before. Relay verbatim.
- Fasting, if any: from what time, and whether water is allowed.
- Whether they need somebody to take them home.
- What to wear or remove.

## The one thing to add unprompted

**Whether they can drive afterwards.** People plan around it and rarely ask.

## Hand over

Any question about whether *their* medication counts as one to stop. That is
specific to their prescription and belongs with a clinician, however clearly
the sheet appears to answer it.

## Never

Say a preparation step can be skipped, or estimate how long a procedure will
take from experience rather than the sheet.
